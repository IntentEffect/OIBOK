# Versions and corrections

## Blog article expansions | 23 November 2026

The three launch articles have been expanded to explain the ideas more fully and make their practical use clearer. [Why OIBOK exists](/blog/why-oibok-exists/) develops the organisational gap and the connections between knowledge, decisions and lasting capability. [From AI tools to organisational capability](/blog/from-ai-tools-to-organisational-capability/) examines the work around an AI task, including responsibilities, handoffs and evaluation. [Start with one workflow](/blog/start-with-one-workflow/) adds more guidance for using the tools with a recurring workflow.

Each article includes a dated expansion note and is recorded as Article 1.1. Original publication dates are retained. OIBOK research release 1.0 and Practical Toolkit 0.1 remain the underlying sources.

## Blog launch | 23 November 2026

The [OIBOK blog](/blog/) opens with three editorial articles: [Why OIBOK exists](/blog/why-oibok-exists/), [From AI tools to organisational capability](/blog/from-ai-tools-to-organisational-capability/), and [Start with one workflow](/blog/start-with-one-workflow/). They develop earlier project writing, positioning discussions and practical toolkit guidance into standalone articles, with sources and adaptation notes.

The section includes article dates, author credits, an [RSS feed](/blog/feed.xml), navigation links and [contribution guidance](/contribute/#blog-articles). Articles are searchable and appear in the agent source catalogue as editorial material. This addition does not amend the OIBOK 1.0 research release or change Toolkit 0.1.

## Agent guidance 0.1 | 22 November 2026

The [For agents guide](/for-agents/) explains how an assistant or agent can apply OIBOK to an organisational question, select relevant chapters, assess evidence, compare explanations and produce a practical tool or action record. It includes a starter prompt and guidance on citation, authority and retained learning.

The same source is available as [Markdown instructions](/agents/instructions.md). Generated [discovery guidance](/llms.txt) and a [source catalogue](/agents/manifest.json) provide exact document links, publication and component versions, content hashes and deployment commit. The footer, Tools page and repository README link to the guide.

This is a website and practice addition. It does not amend the preserved OIBOK 1.0 research sources, release the proposed 1.1 revision or validate the model or toolkit. Original publication content remains under CC BY-SA 4.0 with the existing attribution and exceptions.

## Website and practical toolkit | 22 November 2026

This website brings the existing OIBOK research release and the practical toolkit into one publication repository. It adds readable chapter pages, searchable text, downloadable resources and public contribution guidance.

The website reads the Markdown publication files directly. The introduction, navigation and research policies are new editorial material. The underlying research chapter files and archived release downloads remain unchanged.

### Practical toolkit 0.1

The toolkit introduces a Practice Review, an AI Opportunity and Work Design Canvas, and an Outcome and Learning Record. Supporting Context and Knowledge and Decision and Coordination canvases connect the activities. Download the [five A3 canvases, editable documents and guidance](/tools/).

These are resources for piloting in practice. They are not a validated organisational intelligence score, certification scheme or guarantee of improved performance.

## OIBOK research release 1.0 | 15 August 2026

The research release contains ten chapters and supporting terminology, proposition, diagnostic, traceability, open-question and source registers. It is a literature-grounded working synthesis.

Chapter files retain component version 0.3 and their historical candidate metadata. The [completion record](/research/release-record/) is the release authority that promotes those unchanged files into OIBOK 1.0. A file component version and a publication edition describe different things.

The [research section](/research/) provides the source archives and supporting registers. The consistency proof concerns internal reconciliation and source traceability. It does not establish empirical validation of the whole OIBOK model.

## Proposed 1.1 revision

An editorial amendment proposal dated 21 November 2026 remains under consideration. Its suggested changes have not been applied to the 1.0 chapter files on this site. A future research release needs a recorded editorial decision, reconciled registers and clear release notes.
