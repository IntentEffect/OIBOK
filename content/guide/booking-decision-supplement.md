# Worked decision: a booking handoff

**Web supplement to Guide 0.6 · 25 November 2026.** The booking cases, figures and decision below are fictional. A service team is deciding whether to expand a trial in which the scheduler explicitly accepts each ready job or returns it with a question.

| Fictional trial record | Previous 10 eligible bookings | Next 10 eligible bookings |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Customers given a booking date within two working days | 6 of 10 | 8 of 10 |
| Median staff time spent checking a booking | 4 minutes | 7 minutes |
| Jobs requiring the specialist technician | 5 of 10 | 2 of 10 |

**What happened at the handoff?** In one delayed trial case the scheduler recorded the acceptance the same morning and named the technician needed. The next suitable slot was three days away. Another notification would have done little for that customer. In a second case sales marked the job ready, but the shared queue showed no receiving owner until the customer rang. A missed handoff remains a credible explanation for that delay. [Evidence on selective attention](/research/integrated-evidence-notes/#02--selective-attention) · [Evidence on connection quality](/research/integrated-evidence-notes/#09--quality-of-the-connections).

**What did the team learn?** The usual scheduler used the new routine on eight bookings. Their cover handled the other two and needed a private prompt from sales on one. Updating the instructions gave the team a record. The cover shift exposed a gap in using it. [Evidence on persistent learning](/research/integrated-evidence-notes/#07--persistent-learning).

**Decision recorded by the service manager: adapt and extend the limited trial.** More customers received dates promptly, but this group also had fewer specialist jobs and checking took longer. The scheduling lead will add a visible owner for returned jobs. For the next ten eligible bookings, the lead will record specialist availability, handoff acceptance, customer wait and checking time. A cover worker will handle cases using the shared instructions. At the tenth booking the service manager will compare like cases and review any extra work with both teams. Continue locally if the handoff holds on cover shifts at an acceptable cost. Adjust the routine if work is shifting upstream. Stop it if the extra checking persists without a customer benefit.
