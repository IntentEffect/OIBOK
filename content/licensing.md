## What the publication licence covers

The licence covers original OIBOK publication text, original diagrams, editorial documentation, practical guides, blank templates and worked examples. Material carrying a separate notice follows that notice. Software, names and official brand assets have the separate treatment described below.

## What you can do

You can copy, share, translate, adapt and commercially use licensed material. When sharing it, give appropriate credit, link to the licence and indicate changes. When sharing an adaptation, apply CC BY-SA 4.0 or a compatible licence as permitted by the legal code. Do not impose additional legal or technological restrictions that prevent recipients exercising the licensed rights.

The licence does not require you to publish private adaptations or completed organisational worksheets. You can use the tools internally and keep your records in an appropriately controlled environment. If you share adapted template content, follow the applicable licence terms.

The licence does not imply endorsement. It also does not override rights in material that OIBOK does not own, or other rights such as privacy and publicity rights. Exceptions and limitations under applicable law continue to apply.

## How to give credit

Retain relevant supplied copyright and licence notices, credit named authors or contributors where provided, link to the source and licence, and describe changes. The following is a suggested format:

> Organisation Intelligence Body of Knowledge (OIBOK), [resource or chapter title], [version], [source URL]. Founded by Intent & Effect, developed by the OIBOK community. Licensed under CC BY-SA 4.0. [Description of changes, if any.]

You may use another reasonable attribution format that meets the licence. The suggested wording does not add a separate licensing condition.

The project's attribution model is:

**Founded by Intent & Effect, developed by the OIBOK community.**

## Contributor copyright

Contributors retain copyright in their original work. They grant a non-exclusive CC BY-SA 4.0 licence for original publication contributions intentionally submitted for inclusion. They can continue using their work elsewhere; no copyright assignment to Intent & Effect is required.

The [contribution policy](/contribute/) explains the declaration, permissions, disclosures and review process. Applying a licence to a contribution does not give the project rights in unrelated material owned by that contributor.

## Third-party sources and assets

Cited articles, figures, datasets, photographs, logos, embedded media and excerpts retain their own rights. A citation or link does not bring the source under OIBOK's publication licence. Check the source's terms and any exception notice before reusing such material.

OIBOK and Intent & Effect names, logos and other official brand assets are excluded from the publication licence. CC BY-SA 4.0 does not grant trademark rights. You can identify the source of material in appropriate attribution, but an independent adaptation must not imply official status or endorsement.

The Creative Commons badge is used to identify the publication licence and remains subject to [Creative Commons' policies](https://creativecommons.org/policies/).

## Website software

A separate licence for original website software has not yet been adopted. Publication permissions do not extend to software merely because it shares this repository. Third-party software retains its own licence.

This distinction applies to build scripts, templates, stylesheets, client scripts and deployment configuration. It does not change the CC BY-SA 4.0 permissions for the original publication content those files display.

## Licence continuity

The initial repository skeleton was made available under CC0 1.0 Universal. Its existing CC0 permissions remain in effect for that previously covered material. The publication licence applied to newly added OIBOK content does not withdraw or retroactively restrict those permissions. The historical CC0 notice is retained in [the repository](https://github.com/IntentEffect/OIBOK/blob/main/LICENSES/CC0-1.0.txt) and its version history.

A valid CC BY-SA 4.0 grant also continues for distributed material while its conditions are followed. A later policy change does not cancel permissions already granted. Future relicensing of contributed work may require permission from the relevant copyright holders.

For an unclear rights notice or a missing source credit, open a [repository issue](https://github.com/IntentEffect/OIBOK/issues) identifying the resource and concern. Keep confidential information out of public submissions.
