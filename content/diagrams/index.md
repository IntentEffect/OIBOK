# OIBOK diagrams

**DRAFT 0.1 · For practitioner review · 23 November 2026**

Visual explanations for applying organisational intelligence to real work. Explore the model, investigate a problem, design how people and AI work together, evaluate a change and examine whether useful learning lasts.

The collection contains five practical diagrams, three supporting visuals and a readable overview of the existing OIBOK model. Each has an accessible text explanation, source links, an editable SVG and an A3 landscape PDF.

## Read the diagrams in context

The diagrams explain the Guide and the current research synthesis. They are drafts for practitioner review, with no validated maturity score or certification. Practice routes can be revisited. Local workflow results support bounded claims. Fictional examples and planning assumptions provide no empirical evidence.

OIBOK release 1.0 and Practical Toolkit 0.1 remain the source editions. The illustrated Guide and Working Companion are draft 0.4. The existing interactive model remains draft 0.2.
