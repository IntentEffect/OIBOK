# Explore the OIBOK model

**DRAFT 0.2 · For practitioner review · 23 November 2026.** This visual explains the current OIBOK working model. It is published for feedback on clarity, interpretation and practical usefulness. It is based on OIBOK research release 1.0 and does not amend that release. Independent field validation of the complete model remains pending.

Select a capability or connecting element to explore its purpose, activities, possible evidence, people and AI contributions, illustrative breakdowns and practical questions. Each section links to the relevant research chapters and practical tools.

[Browse the practical diagram collection](/diagrams/) for the overview, work-design examples, evaluation and learning figures, with SVG and A3 PDF downloads.

[Read the complete model reference](/model/reference/) for all the detail in an accessible reading format, or open the [OIBOK Guide (draft 0.5)](/guide/read/) to work through an organisational problem.

The three transformations are recurring and overlapping. The six capabilities have equal status. Contextualise, organisational orientation and the Outcome–Feedback bridge explain connections and states within the model. Organisational memory, purpose, governance and responsible exercise apply across the work. AI participation is optional and bounded by organisational authority. [Chapter 10](/knowledge/integrated-and-responsible-organisational-intelligence/) contains the governing definitions, research argument and limitations.
