# OIBOK Blog

Ideas and practical guidance for understanding organisational work and making worthwhile changes with AI.

The blog explores why OIBOK exists, how its ideas relate to everyday decisions, and how to use the practical tools. Articles are editorial perspectives and guides. The [research publication](https://intenteffect.github.io/OIBOK/knowledge/) provides the underlying chapters, evidence and stated limitations.
