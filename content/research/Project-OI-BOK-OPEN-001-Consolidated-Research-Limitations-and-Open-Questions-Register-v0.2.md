## 1. Purpose

This artefact consolidates the research limitations and unresolved questions remaining after the nine Project OI domain chapters and the first consolidation artefacts.

Its governing question is:

> **What remains unknown, conceptually unsettled, empirically untested, difficult to measure, normatively unresolved or temporally unstable before Project OI can present one integrated and responsible account of Organisational Intelligence?**

The register exists to prevent three opposite failures.

### False closure

The literature-grounded Body of Knowledge could be presented as though its constructs, propositions and indicators had already been validated across organisations.

### Unbounded deferral

Every limitation could be treated as a reason to avoid completing a coherent first release.

### Silent resolution

Chapter 10 could remove uncertainty by choosing preferred language or mechanisms without preserving the fact that the source material did not settle them.

The required approach is:

```text
Preserve the exact source limitation
        ↓
Classify the type of unresolved issue
        ↓
Form one integrated open question where appropriate
        ↓
Decide what must be resolved in Chapter 10
        ↓
Decide what belongs in adversarial review
        ↓
Preserve empirical questions for post-BOK validation
        ↓
Maintain AI-era claims continuously
```

## 2. Interpretation Boundary

This register contains two different kinds of content.

### Source-derived open records

These preserve the limitations, terminology questions, validation questions, transition questions, known gaps and review requirements stated by the existing BOK artefacts.

Their wording is retained in the machine-readable source register.

### Project OI integrated open questions

These combine several source limitations into a smaller cross-chapter question that can guide OI-BOK-010, the adversarial review and later empirical research.

They are **Project OI synthesis**, not direct statements from one source chapter.

### What an open item means

An open item does not imply that:

- the BOK is incoherent;
- the relevant chapter is unusable;
- the construct should be removed;
- or the issue must be empirically solved before the first coherent BOK release.

It means that the item requires one of the following dispositions:

1. **Resolve in OI-BOK-010** through explicit construct design and bounded wording.
2. **Test in OI-BOK-REVIEW-001** through construct-validity and adversarial review.
3. **Preserve as a release limitation** where the current evidence cannot settle it.
4. **Defer to post-BOK empirical research** where field evidence is required.
5. **Maintain continuously** where AI, standards or technology can change materially.

## 6. Consolidated Conclusion

The source base supports completion of OI-BOK-010 as a **coherent working synthesis**.

It does not support presenting Project OI as:

- an empirically validated general theory;
- a validated diagnostic instrument;
- a causal model proven across organisational settings;
- a single maturity score;
- a universal governance prescription;
- or a stable theory of AI capability independent of model, task, interface, provider and date.

No current limitation requires an eleventh substantive chapter.

The open issues divide into four practical groups.

### Must be dispositioned in OI-BOK-010

- the final construct boundary;
- the three transformations and six operational capabilities;
- the status of Contextualise, Orientation and Outcome–Feedback;
- Connection Quality;
- the unit and level of analysis;
- the relationship between instrumental capability and responsible exercise;
- purpose, legitimacy, ethics and power;
- and the evidence architecture for bounded OI claims.

### Must be challenged in OI-BOK-REVIEW-001

- working terms;
- construct overlap;
- circularity;
- normative language;
- chapter and transition consistency;
- and whether the final model is parsimonious.

### Must remain open after the first BOK release

- empirical generalisability;
- causal validity;
- longitudinal loop closure;
- field and SMB validity;
- measurement reliability and discriminant validity;
- and capability/outcome prediction.

### Must be maintained continuously

- AI- and Agent-related capability claims;
- security and identity conditions;
- model and provider behaviour;
- evaluation methods;
- and standards or regulatory assumptions.

## 7. Integrated Open Questions

The following **28 integrated questions** form the primary open-question architecture for Chapter 10 and the final review.


### Construct and boundary

#### OI-OPEN-IQ-001 — Distinctiveness of Organisational Intelligence

> **Is Organisational Intelligence sufficiently distinct from collective intelligence, organisational learning, knowledge management, coordination, dynamic capability, information processing, culture, performance and AI capability to justify one coherent construct?**

**Why it remains open:** The chapters integrate several established literatures; integration can create construct inflation or relabelling without discriminant validity.

**Source basis:** TRACE known gaps; V1; all chapter construct limitations; GLO critical distinctions

**Priority:** P0

**Primary target:** OI-BOK-010 and OI-BOK-REVIEW-001

**Minimum disposition or evidence:** State necessary and non-necessary components, adjacent-construct boundaries, counterexamples and claims not made.

**After the first BOK release:** Requires later empirical discriminant-validity research.

#### OI-OPEN-IQ-002 — Parsimony of the three transformations and six capabilities

> **Are Orient, Coordinate and Improve, implemented through Sense, Know, Choose, Mobilise, Learn and Adapt, the most parsimonious architecture for the source material?**

**Why it remains open:** The model is a Project OI synthesis rather than an established taxonomy under the same labels.

**Source basis:** SYN roadmap decisions; PROP O-class propositions; TRACE V1–V3

**Priority:** P0

**Primary target:** OI-BOK-010 and OI-BOK-REVIEW-001

**Minimum disposition or evidence:** Demonstrate full coverage, non-redundancy, semantic ownership and why alternative decompositions are less useful.

**After the first BOK release:** Requires field and comparative construct testing.

#### OI-OPEN-IQ-003 — Status of Contextualise, Orientation and Outcome–Feedback

> **Should Contextualise remain a connective mechanism, Orientation an integrative state and Outcomes–Feedback a bridge rather than operational capabilities?**

**Why it remains open:** These constructs are essential but do not have equivalent chapter or capability status.

**Source basis:** SYN RD-003/RD-004; TRACE stage and gap registers; GLO open decisions

**Priority:** P0

**Primary target:** OI-BOK-010

**Minimum disposition or evidence:** Define each object, owner, inputs, outputs and non-capability status without weakening the end-to-end model.

**After the first BOK release:** Retain as a construct-design question for later empirical work.

#### OI-OPEN-IQ-004 — Connection Quality

> **Is Connection Quality a distinct and useful integrative construct, and how can it be observed without becoming a universal score?**

## 8. Chapter-Level Limitation Synthesis

The full exact limitation wording is preserved in the source register. The following section summarises what each chapter leaves unresolved.

### OI-BOK-001 — Signals and Attention

The chapter does not establish a validated Project OI attention construct, complete failure taxonomy, causal indicator model or assessment instrument. Its signal-to-attention lifecycle and terms such as Attentional Debt and Orphaned Attention remain working syntheses.

The principal open issues are:

- operationalising organisational attention without reducing it to message volume or visibility;
- validating whether the proposed signal lifecycle is complete;
- defining Sense quality despite the absence of a source quality-dimension section;
- and testing the model in real organisational settings.

### OI-BOK-002 — Organisational Context

The chapter does not establish a validated context model, mutually exclusive taxonomy, safe boundary for representing tacit context, causal indicator model or context-quality score.

Its working terms require later challenge, particularly:

- Context Decay;
- Context Drift;
- Context Laundering;
- Evidence-Bearing Context;
- and Minimum Sufficient Context.

### OI-BOK-003 — Knowledge and Expertise

The chapter leaves open:

- measurement of expertise and expertise coordination;
- boundaries among knowledge, knowing, skill, competence and capability;
- completeness of the routing process;
- level-of-analysis relations among individual expertise, team transactive memory and organisational knowledge;
- the tacit–explicit boundary;
- causal direction among knowledge access, coordination and performance;
- AI generalisability;
- and the ethical legitimacy of the purpose to which expertise is applied.

### OI-BOK-004 — Decision and Authority

The chapter does not provide one validated decision model or universal allocation method for decision rights.

It preserves open questions concerning:

- diversity among routine, strategic, emergency, legal and moral decisions;
- relations among individual judgement, team deliberation, organisational authority and institutional legitimacy;
- hidden power and coercion;
- cultural and legal variation;
- separation of process and outcome;
- whether AI can meaningfully be described as making a decision;
- and whether decision quality can be measured without one reductive score.

### OI-BOK-005 — Coordination and Collective Action

The chapter does not establish a validated coordination model or one universal mechanism-selection method.

It preserves questions concerning:

- competing disciplinary definitions;
- level of analysis from pairs to multi-Agent systems;
- power and burden shifting through dependency design;
- invisible relationship and repair work;
- cultural variation;
- causal direction between coordination and performance;
- AI-team evidence generalisability;
- organisational equivalence of technical multi-Agent research;
- and the distinction between strong coordination and legitimate purpose.

### OI-BOK-006 — Execution and Operational Reliability

The chapter leaves open:

- cross-disciplinary translation of engineering dependability terms;
- contested service definitions;
- level-of-analysis differences;
- generalisation from high-reliability and hazardous settings to ordinary SMB work;

## 13. Disposition and Closure Model

### Closure criteria

An item is not closed merely because it has disappeared from the prose.

It is closed for the first BOK release only where:

- a preferred definition or boundary is stated;
- the source uncertainty remains traceable;
- the strength of claim is appropriate;
- the residual empirical question is preserved where necessary;
- and the disposition is recorded in OI-BOK-REVIEW-001 or OI-BOK-RELEASE-001.

## 14. Minimum Post-BOK Empirical Programme

The following is a Project OI roadmap recommendation derived from the unresolved questions. It is not a source finding.

### 14.1 Comparative organisational case studies

Study a purposive set of organisations including:

- small owner-led businesses;
- growing multi-team SMBs;
- professional or research organisations;
- public or regulated organisations;
- and larger enterprises.

The objective is to test:

- construct boundaries;
- unit of analysis;
- contextual variation;
- and whether the six capabilities and transitions are observable.

### 14.2 Longitudinal episode tracing

Trace material organisational episodes from:

```text
Signal
→ attention
→ context
→ knowledge
→ decision
→ coordination
→ execution
→ outcome
→ learning
→ adaptation
```

This should examine:

- where conversions fail;
- which transitions are bypassed;
- how memory changes later reconstruction;
- and whether adaptation changes the next episode.

### 14.3 Transition and handoff studies

Test the provisional Connection Quality construct through:

- routing;
- handoff;
- authority;
- execution;
- evaluation;
- and learning boundaries.

### 14.4 Measurement research

Develop and test an evidence portfolio rather than one maturity score.

The research should examine:

- discriminant validity;
- convergent validity;
- reliability;
- predictive value;
- proxy risk;
- and metric reactivity.

### 14.5 SMB field tests

Test whether the model can be applied proportionately without enterprise bureaucracy.

The field work should examine:

- founder dependency;
- tacit knowledge;
- informal authority;
- low-data evaluation;
- capability concentration;
- and external provider dependence.

### 14.6 Human–AI comparative studies

Compare:

- human-only;
- AI-assisted;
- automated;
- and Agent-mediated

arrangements at the level of:

- task;
- Workflow;
- organisational outcome;
- human learning;
- authority;
- resilience;
- and capability persistence.

### 14.7 Counterexample and falsification studies

Identify organisations that:

- perform strongly without some proposed capability;
- possess the proposed capability without strong outcomes;
- or appear highly adaptive while failing responsible-purpose criteria.

A credible construct should state what evidence would count against it.
