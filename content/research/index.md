# Research and evidence

OIBOK is a working synthesis that can be inspected, challenged and revised. The current research baseline is **Release 1.0, dated 15 August 2026**. Its chapters and supporting records distinguish conceptual claims, evidence, proposed explanations and unresolved questions.

## What the research currently supports

The release provides a bounded definition, a proposed operational model and a connected account of organisational response and learning. It brings established research into a common structure and identifies questions for empirical investigation.

It does not establish that the whole model predicts organisational performance, that its capabilities are necessary or sufficient, or that its diagnostic concepts form a validated assessment instrument. Internal consistency checks are distinct from field validation.

## Source records

The source occurrence crosswalk records what each chapter cites. The unique-source register is provisional because compound entries and title-based matching can over-count or under-count individual works. The release reports that older references were structurally reconciled, but not every older source was independently reopened at its publisher. These limitations remain relevant when using the evidence.

## How new findings enter OIBOK

Research updates should explain what changed, assess evidence quality, consider competing explanations and map implications to the owning chapter and related records. New evidence may strengthen, qualify or contradict the current synthesis. A new paper is not automatically a reason to revise the model.

The [research methodology](/methodology/) sets out the ongoing editorial procedure, including source verification, AI assistance, review and change control. The procedure is an editorial standard and is not a claim that every historical chapter followed a fully systematic review protocol.

A proposed 1.1 revision remains under editorial consideration. It is not presented here as a released edition.
