## 2. Governing Definition

### Short Definition

> **Organisational Intelligence is the persistent organisation-level capacity to form an orientation from relevant signals and experience, coordinate collective response, and improve future response through feedback.**

The concise wording should be interpreted through the detailed definition and boundary notes in OI-BOK-010 v0.3.

### Extended Definition

> **Organisational Intelligence is the persistent capacity of an organisational system to convert signals and experience judged material under current purpose and context into a sufficiently shared or aligned orientation, convert that orientation into a deliberate and authorised organisationally enacted response, and use consequences and feedback to strengthen or preserve future response fitness under stated conditions. The capacity is instantiated through the organisation’s people, relationships, structures, practices, technologies and external ties rather than being reducible to the intelligence of any one participant, model, system or episode. Project OI hypothesises that the complete capacity depends materially on the integrity of the transitions among its capabilities.**

The transition statement is a research hypothesis. Connection Quality remains a provisional diagnostic lens rather than a validated constitutive dimension or score.

### Responsible Exercise

> **The responsible exercise of Organisational Intelligence requires epistemic quality proportionate to risk, legitimate authority, accountability, contestable purpose, appropriate representation of affected interests, respect for applicable rights and law, and human and technical controls proportionate to autonomy, uncertainty, reversibility and consequence.**

The release retains one descriptive OI construct.

It distinguishes whether that capacity is responsibly or irresponsibly governed.

## 3. Released Model

```text
ORIENT
        ↓
COORDINATE
        ↓
IMPROVE
```

supported operationally by:

```text
SENSE
        ↓
CONTEXTUALISE
        ↓
KNOW
        ↓
ORGANISATIONAL ORIENTATION
        ↓
CHOOSE
        ↓
MOBILISE — COORDINATION
        ↓
MOBILISE — EXECUTION
        ↓
OUTCOME AND FEEDBACK
        ↓
LEARN
        ↓
ADAPT
```

The three transformations are the parsimonious construct architecture.

The six capabilities are the current operational model.

Contextualise and Outcome–Feedback are connective mechanisms.

Organisational Orientation is an integrated state.

The model is recursive, nested and context-sensitive. It is not one universal workflow or validated factor structure.
