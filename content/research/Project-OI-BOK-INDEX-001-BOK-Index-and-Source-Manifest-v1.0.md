## 6. Evidence and Source Boundary

The release contains:

```text
338 exact citation occurrences
290 provisionally normalised source records
18 governed time-sensitive AI and AI-agent evidence records
```

The occurrence crosswalk remains the authoritative record of what each chapter cites. The 290-source count remains provisional because compound entries and title-based deduplication can under- or over-count works.

Stable older references were structurally normalised and reconciled; not every older source was independently reopened at its publisher during CORR-002. Current and anomalous AI-era evidence was checked within the scope recorded in the bibliographic and lifecycle registers.

---

## 7. Release Boundary

Release 1.0 means that Project OI has a coherent, bounded, traceable working BOK.

It does not mean that the construct is:

- empirically validated;
- a proven general theory;
- a validated diagnostic instrument;
- a maturity model;
- a single score;
- validated for SMBs;
- or implemented canonically by a software product.

The complete permitted-claim and limitation boundary is controlled by OI-BOK-RELEASE-001 and its claims register.

---

## 8. Change Control

A material source-chapter change should propagate through:

```text
Owning chapter
        ↓
Glossary
        ↓
Proposition, diagnostic and trace registers
        ↓
Open-question and source registers
        ↓
Index, validation and release record
```

Machine-readable registers remain derived artefacts and do not override the narrative source chapters.
