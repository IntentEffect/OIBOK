# Review one recurring problem

[Download the one-page A4 worksheet (fillable PDF)](/downloads/toolkit/OIBOK-Two-Case-Review-Draft-0.1-A4.pdf).

Use this draft with someone who does the work, someone who receives it and someone who can authorise a small, reversible change. The same person can hold more than one role. Bring one recent case that went well and one that stalled. Keep the completed record with your team.

## Before you read the chapter

Write down the team’s current explanation and what you already planned to do. Keeping that first answer gives you something to compare with your decision afterwards. Then read [Signals and attention](/knowledge/signals-and-attention/reader/) and follow both cases from the first useful clue to the next decision.

## Copy these prompts into your work notes

| Prompt | Your entry |
| --- | --- |
| What recurring problem are we examining? | Where does the work begin and end? Who feels the effect? |
| What do we already think, and what would we do? | Record the original explanation and intended action before reading further. |
| Case that went well | Case reference, first clue, who received it, what they did and the observed result. |
| Case that stalled | Record the same details. Use the evidence available at the time. |
| Which explanation fits? | What else could account for the difference? What would change your mind? |
| What did the review add? | Record what changed in your explanation or intended action. “Nothing” is a useful answer. |
| What will we do next? | Try a small change, investigate further, or keep current practice. Who owns the decision? When will you review it? |
| At the review date | What happened on the next eligible cases? Include extra work or harm. Did the change hold with a different colleague? Decide whether to continue, adapt or stop. |

If authority is unclear, the change is difficult to reverse, or several teams would carry the consequences, take the question into the [full Practice Review](/tools/practice-review/). For a selected change that needs a fuller comparison, use the [Outcome and Learning Record](/tools/outcome-and-learning/). If the team is testing this worksheet, record the actual time spent and follow the [field pilot brief](/research/chapter-one-pilot/).
