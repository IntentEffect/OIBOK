# Practical tools

Start with one recurring decision or workflow. Use the OIBOK toolkit to investigate a problem, compare possible changes and examine the effects. The aim is a documented action and a record of what happened.

**Practical Toolkit 0.1, 22 November 2026.** These are practice resources for piloting. They do not provide a validated intelligence score, maturity measure or certification.

## Choose a starting point

**Working with an AI assistant?** [Use these tools with an AI assistant or agent](/for-agents/) for a starter prompt and a method for selecting sources, comparing explanations and drafting a practical working record.

| Resource | Use it to | Guidance |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Organisational Intelligence Practice Review | Identify the problem, evidence, unknowns and a worthwhile improvement. | [Read the review and worked example](/tools/practice-review/) |
| Context and Knowledge Map | Examine the signals, context and knowledge needed for this specific work. | [Included in the Practice Review](/tools/practice-review/) |
| Decision and Coordination Canvas | Make decision rights, dependencies, handoffs and exceptions visible. | [Included in the Practice Review](/tools/practice-review/) |
| AI Opportunity and Work Design Canvas | Compare AI and other options, then specify a bounded intervention. | [Read the canvas and worked example](/tools/ai-opportunity-and-work-design/) |
| Outcome and Learning Record | Plan evaluation before the change, review effects and retain learning. | [Read the record and worked example](/tools/outcome-and-learning/) |

The tools can be revisited as understanding changes. If the proposed intervention contains no AI, connect the Practice Review directly to the Outcome and Learning Record.

## Use the tools with evidence

Bring contrasting cases, current guidance and available outcome data. Distinguish observations, interpretations and unknowns. Complete the evaluation plan before changing the work. Assign an owner and a review date, then check later whether improvements persist.

The Harbour Services worked example is fictional. Its records, counts, targets and results are instructional examples, not evidence of organisational outcomes. These resources were drafted with AI assistance and checked for source consistency and layout; independent editorial review and field evaluation remain future steps.

Read the [toolkit guide](/tools/guide/) for preparation, evidence conventions, chapter mapping and source notes.
