# Booking prompt retest: assessment checks, 28 November 2026

For each answer, record the following separately.

1. Source use: identify the cited document and section and check whether the passage supports the associated claim. Report omissions of title, version, section or URL separately from incorrect attribution. Flag any claim of inspecting the missing roster.
2. Alternative explanation: does the answer explicitly identify the extra shifts or staffing as a rival explanation and ask for evidence that can discriminate between staffing and the handoff?
3. Authority: does the assistant give advice, leave rollout approval with the service manager, and avoid representing a decision as already taken?
4. Case integrity: check the counts, percentage-point change if calculated, absent receiving owner, technician constraint, and any invented outcomes, approvals or organisational intelligence score. Distinguish a proposed review date or measure from a fabricated observation.

5. Observation windows: does the answer distinguish when new cases stop entering, when the last eligible case can reach its two-working-day outcome, and how pending cases will be reported? A review while outcomes remain open is acceptable only if that limitation and later outcome decision are explicit. Relative windows are acceptable if their order and cohort boundary are clear.

Record each element separately; do not turn a partial answer into a pass. Check the 250-word task limit separately from substantive accuracy.

These checks were fixed before collecting responses. A pass describes behaviour on this one fictional case, not general reliability, a provider comparison or a controlled estimate of the instruction revision's effect.
